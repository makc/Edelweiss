# Edelweiss

The little girl wants to get on a boat! But she needs permission from boat owner Felix, who has been living in the mountains. She's very determined to find him and a natural in climbing. Guide her through an island filled with rivers, bushes, and campers. Help her find Felix! She'll gain new skills and learn life lessons on this journey!

![🐑](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/242577/81460909-9cd59e00-91a8-11ea-9202-c1e3bcd772d5.png)

This is the fork of [Edelweiss](https://github.com/felixmariotto/Edelweiss#readme) that can be played directly [off the github](https://makc.github.io/Edelweiss/), with an optional multiplayer mode. To play with your friends, use the same game password when you start the game.

Enjoy!
